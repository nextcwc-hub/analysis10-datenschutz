2. Summary

  • The app requires no user account and no login.
  • All settings and learning progress are stored exclusively locally on your device.
  • There are no analytics or tracking services, no advertising, no crash reports and no servers of our own. We do not receive any data from you.
  • All content (lessons, exercises, formulas) is included in the app; it works offline.
  • Purchases are processed via Google Play.

3. Data stored locally

To create a personal study plan, the app stores the following on your device: course level, optionally your federal state, exam date, desired study time or target date, selected additional topics, as well as your learning progress and results (e.g. completed units, solved exercises, mistake log, mock exam results, learning streak). The start of the free trial period is also stored locally. This data does not leave your device and is not transmitted to us or to third parties. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (provision of the app's functions).

You can delete your progress at any time in the app under Einstellungen → Fortschritt zurücksetzen (Settings → Reset progress). Uninstalling the app removes all data completely.

4. Purchases via Google Play

5. Google Play Store

When you download the app from the Google Play Store, Google processes data such as your Google account and device data. We have no influence on this; Google's privacy policy applies.

6. Children and adolescents

The app is aimed at school students. Since we do not collect or receive any personal data, no data from minors is transmitted to us either.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Since we do not store any data about you ourselves, you can delete the locally stored data directly in the app. If you have any questions, please contact us at the email address above.
